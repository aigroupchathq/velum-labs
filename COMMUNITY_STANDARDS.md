## 1. The Core Philosophy of Check Community Safety

Check is designed as an intentional relational sanctuary. We reject gamification, predatory swiping, and disposable communication cultures. Every member agrees to uphold psychological safety, emotional transparency, and rigorous consent.

## 2. Zero-Tolerance Violations (Immediate Ban & Law Enforcement Referral)

Commission of any of the following acts results in immediate permanent expulsion, forfeiture of all fees, and cryptographic preservation of audit trails for law enforcement subpoena:

### 2.1. Cyberflashing (UK Online Safety Act Section 66 Offence)
- Sending unsolicited photographs, videos, or visual depictions of nudity, genitalia, or sexual acts.
- Check maintains an automated zero-trust media policy: image ingress channels strictly quarantine unmoderated photos.

### 2.2. Romance Scams & Financial Fraud
- Requesting money, wire transfers, cryptocurrency, gift cards, or financial favors under any pretext (including medical emergencies, travel distress, or investment schemes).
- Promoting commercial ventures, multi-level marketing, third-party subscription channels, or sex work.

### 2.3. Minor Infiltration & Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
- Any attempt by an individual under the age of 18 to access Check, or any communication targeting or referencing minors, results in immediate automated notification to the **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)** and the UK **Child Exploitation and Online Protection Command (CEOP)**.

### 2.4. Stalking, Threats & Harassment
- Persistent communication after a member has communicated disinterest, executed a safety block, or initiated a closure handshake.
- Threats of violence, suicide coercion, blackmail, or intimidation.

### 2.5. Non-Consensual Doxxing & Disclosure of Private Tier 4 Data
- Exposing, screenshotting, or publicly disseminating any member's profile, photos, neurodivergent sensory thresholds, medical accommodations, or private values outside the platform.

## 3. Pacing, Respect & Mutual Closure (The Gentle Handshake)

3.1. **Closure Handshake Protocol:** When an interaction has run its course, Check encourages the use of our pre-built, dignified **Closure Handshake** rather than abrupt ghosting. A closure handshake politely concludes the dialogue, locks message threads, and cleanly archives the dyad without acrimony.

3.2. **Instant Bidirectional Safety Blocks (Invariant S-01):**
- When a user blocks another member, the block is **instantaneous, symmetric, and bidirectional**.
- Both profiles immediately vanish from each other's candidate slates, active chats, and search results.
- Anti-self-block integrity prevents malicious self-targeting, and unblock commands are permanently auditable.

## 4. In-Person Date Guidelines (Encounter Guide)

When transitioning from digital dialogue to physical encounters ("First Date Brief"):
1. **Public Venues Only:** Select sensory-friendly, low-stimulus cafes, bookstores, museum galleries, or botanical gardens.
2. **Transportation Autonomy:** Arrive and leave via your own transport.
3. **Safety Buddy:** Share venue and match details with a friend.
4. **Exit Freedom:** If at any point you feel uncomfortable, you have the right to end the encounter immediately without justification.

## 5. Regional Statutory Reporting & Regulatory Compliance

### 5.1. European Union (Digital Services Act Article 16 & 22)
- EU users and certified Trusted Flaggers may submit priority notices of illegal content or severe harassment directly to `dsa-compliance@check-compatibility.internal`.
- Notices trigger human review within 24 hours and a formal statement of reasons pursuant to DSA Article 17.

### 5.2. United Kingdom (Online Safety Act 2023 & Malicious Communications Act 1988)
- Check fulfills statutory duties of care under the UK OSA 2023 to proactively prevent illegal harms, cyberflashing (s.66 Sexual Offences Act), and grossly offensive communications.
- Reports are escalated to our UK Designated Safety Officer at `safety-uk@check-compatibility.internal`.

### 5.3. Australia (Online Safety Act 2021 & eSafety Commissioner)
- Check complies with the Australian Online Safety Act 2021. Australian members subjected to cyber-abuse or non-consensual image sharing may report directly to Check and simultaneously lodge complaints with the Australian eSafety Commissioner (`esafety.gov.au`).
